title: Welcome to Asterum Academy
---

As [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum-students/khar-lo-flameheart|khar-lo-flameheart]] waits outside the locked gate leading to [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/asterum-academy|asterum-academy]], the day of the autumnal equinox finally arrives. The gate opens, and a man in a fine suit walks up to Khar-Lo and leads him to the academy through a rough yet well maintained tunnel. Khar-Lo exits to the sight of a fine Victorian-style manor, unlike anything she was used to. Brown brick walls, white shining roofs, and a permanence entirely alien to her.

The man goes inside and Khar-Lo looks about the plaza. After looking about for a moment, the man reemerges, this time from an upper level balcony. He introduces himself as [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/augustus-rain|augustus-rain]] of Asterum, and thanks everyone for attending. Participants are told to form groups and seek out the 8 masters of magic that have agreed to participate in this program.

Supplies are distributed, including a map, train pass, and some food. As the commotion in the plaza dies down as all but two remain. Khar-Lo approaches [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/zephiel-gale|zephiel-gale]], and they exchange introductions. Soon after, [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/seraphina-gale|seraphina-gale]] comes out and dotes on Zephiel, worried he will be unable to handle the outside world.

Khar-Lo summons [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum-students/brim|brim]], his golem tiger from the stone of the plaza and concerning Seraphina, and Zephiel tells him about firearms. Khar-Lo tells the tale about his cousin who lost an arm messing with black powder.

Khar-Lo and Zephiel look over the map, reading into finer detail about [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/creblen|creblen]], City of Machines, where they advertise surpassing the power of [[mythos/aurea/people/chimera|chimera]]; [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/sobat|sobat]], City of Might, where they denounce magic and embrace natural strength; [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/posq|posq]], City of Medicine, where there is an alleged elixir of life; and [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/ouldiv|ouldiv]], City of Myths, from where Khar-Lo hails.

The two decide to ignore Creblen due to concerns of hostility toward Khar-Lo, gather some extra supplies for the harsh tundra, and set out south toward Aurkas. There, they plan to take the train to Posq and learn more about the elixir as they challenge the Master.

---

As they depart, they pass by [[mythos/aurea/locations/crater-lake|crater-lake]] and stop to admire the view. As they do, they are attacked by a pack of wolves. The wolves surround Brim, but struggle to bite his stone body, and nearly knock Zephiel into the lake. Khar-Lo eventually fries a few of the wolves with his flame breath and gets tackled by a wolf before Zephiel freezes it cold with a ray of frost from his signet ring. Zephiel heals Khar-Lo and the two of them east some roasted wolf meat.

As night falls, they see a cabin in the distance and decide to head that way. As they travel, a large, mysterious beast passes by them and devours the remaining wolves. The two of them approach the cabin, which sits atop a high cliff ledge. Zephiel notices a mysterious box atop the ledge, but the two of them try to go around.

As they walk up the steep incline, Khar-Lo slips on the hill and slides down to the bottom. He tries to ride Brim up the hill, who also slips and slides down. Defeated, Khar-Lo pauses for a moment and lights a fire while Zephiel looks about. Zephiel is unable to identify the box-shaped device, despite being sure he had seen it before. He pulls the lever on the side anyway, dropping a rope ladder down which Khar-Lo uses to climb up.

Zephiel knocks on the cabin door and is greeted by an old man with a wolf-like appearance. He lets the two of them in while Brim keeps guard outside. A third figure is in the corner sleeping. The man asks where the two of them are going and offers them a drink. Khar-Lo downs it without much effort, while Zephiel, accustomed to finer drinks, gets queasy and passes out.
