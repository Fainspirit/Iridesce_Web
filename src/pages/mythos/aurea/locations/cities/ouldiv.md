Ouldiv is a lush, mountainous region located in central [[mythos/aurea|Aurea]]. Its many villages perch scattered along mountainsides, connected by a winding network of paths through the jungle and bridges spanning over rapids and waterfalls. 

---

Due to its close proximity to [[mythos/aurea/locations/the-abyss|The Abyss]], the concentration of [[mythos/aurea/aura|aura]] is very high. Some residents, such as [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum-students/khar-lo-flameheart|Khar Lo Flameheart]] are able to make use of this abundance of aura to imbue wood, clay, and stone with a semblance of life, creating golems such as [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum-students/brim|Brim]].

Many magic creatures especially near the foot of the mountains.
