title: "chimera"
---
Chimera is the collective term for the demi-human species that live on [[mythos/aurea|aurea]]. Due to their ancestry and genetics, chimeras are naturally attuned to [[mythos/aurea/aura|aura]] and can naturally learn and use magic.
