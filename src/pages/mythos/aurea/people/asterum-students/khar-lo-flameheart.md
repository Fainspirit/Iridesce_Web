Khar-Lo Flameheart is a half-dragon [[mythos/aurea/people/chimera|chimera]] from the [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/ouldiv|Ouldiv]] region. His companion golem [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum-students/brim|Brim]] is always at his side.

Khar-Lo is one of the students invited to [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/asterum-academy|Asterum Academy]].
