Asterum is [[mythos/aurea|Aurea's]] leading [[mythos/aurea/aura|aura]] research organization. They study the flow of aura and how to control it.



---
### Members

- Head Researcher: [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/seraphina-gale|seraphina-gale]]
- Director: [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/augustus-rain|augustus-rain]]

### Inventions

- The [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/technology/conduit|conduit]], a device that allows even a [[mythos/aurea/people/humans|humans]] to focus their aura.
- The [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/technology/vessel|vessel]], an object that can store the magic technique used to cast a spell.
- The [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/technology/channel|channel]], a magitech device that allows users to augment their natural aura through the use of vessels.
