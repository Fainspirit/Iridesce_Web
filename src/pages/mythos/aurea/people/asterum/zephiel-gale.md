Zephiel Gale is a headstrong and unapologetic youth of 19 years. He has brown hair. His mother, [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/seraphina-gale|Seraphina Gale]], founded [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/asterum-academy|Asterum Academy]] for his sake.


---


While Zephiel is fairly well known among [[mythos/aurea/people/humans|humans]] due to their reliance on [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum|Asterum]] technology, he is almost entirely unknown among the less educated [[mythos/aurea/people/chimera|Chimera]].

Zephiel is one of the students invited to [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/asterum-academy|asterum-academy]].
