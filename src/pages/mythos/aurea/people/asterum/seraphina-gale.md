Seraphina is the lead researcher and technical expert for [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum|Asterum]]. She runs [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/asterum-academy|Asterum Academy]] with [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/augustus-rain|Augustus Rain]] and is the inventor of the [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/technology/conduit|conduit]]. Her son, [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/zephiel-gale|Zephiel Gale]], is attending the academy to help her test [[mythos/aurea/people/asterum/technology/chameleon|Chameleon]].

---

She develops technology is meant to improve the lives and situations of [[mythos/aurea/people/humans|humans]]. She bears no ill will toward the [[mythos/aurea/people/chimera|chimera]].
