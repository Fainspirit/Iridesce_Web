Aura is the magical energy that flows within the land of [[mythos/aurea|aurea]]. Aura is a finite resource, similar to matter or energy.

Overuse of Aura can drain the local environment and cause ecological issues. Misuse in <> lead to the foundation of the [[mythos/aurea/locations/cities/aurkas/aurkas-magic-council|Aurkas Magic Council]] - tasked with regulating magic use and reversing the harm done by irresponsible magic use.
